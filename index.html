How to play 6D Chess

Normal chess rules, across a growing multiverse of boards. Each board's past positions hang below it.

  • Cross-timeline move — select any piece except the king. Purple targets on other boards show where it can phase in: it appears on the same square of a board that is at the same move count with you to move, then lands on any empty square it could reach from there.
  • Time travel — queens, rooks, bishops and knights can jump back to an earlier position of their board where it was your move (green portals on the history boards). This creates a new timeline.
  • You can never leave your king in check — on the board you leave or the board you arrive on.
  • The game ends when every timeline has finished; the side with more checkmates wins.
